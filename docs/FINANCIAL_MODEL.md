# Juthoor financial model

Generated by `finance/model.py` (deterministic; `python3 finance/model.py` rewrites `finance/outputs.json`; `tests/test_financial_model.py` checks the arithmetic). All amounts are in JOD, pre-tax.

## Read this first

- The numbers below are **scenario arithmetic, not a forecast**. Demand is unproven: no school has paid for Juthoor.
- The 32-student survey is **early validation of interest only**. It says nothing reliable about schools' willingness to pay, and it was not used to set a price or a volume.
- Inputs are tagged **V** (verified from a public price list or rule, retrieved during the audit phase; re-check before relying on them) or **A** (a team assumption, not evidence). Anything not tagged V is an assumption.
- No market-size figure is used anywhere in the model. Market sizing and competitor facts are deliberately **not** included in the repository because they were not independently verified with dated sources; they live in the separate audit document and must be re-verified before being quoted.

## What the model says, and what it does not

- At 3.5 JOD per student the school plan covers infrastructure and a very small team, but **not** a funded team: with half a salaried support/sales person it needs 17 schools of 250 students (57 schools of 150), and none of the three scenarios recovers the one-time investment within three years.
- At 7.0 JOD the contribution per school is about 3.2x higher (1018 vs 319 JOD at 250 students) and the number of schools needed to break even falls by roughly a factor of three or more. Only the aggressive scenario turns cash-flow positive in year three. **Whether schools will pay 7 JOD is untested**: it must be tested with real quotes before any plan relies on it.
- Teacher and family plans have high unit margins but, in these scenarios, small volumes; they are modelled separately and are not needed for the break-even arithmetic above.
- The model contains no grants, donations, competition prizes or public funding. Churn, discounts and collection are assumptions.
